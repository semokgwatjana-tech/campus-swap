2. Information we collect

We only collect information we need to run the platform and keep it safe:

  • Account details: full name, student number, school email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Optional profile details: course, year of study, campus, bio, and profile picture, if you choose to add them.
  • Marketplace activity: listings you create, messages you send to other students, wishlist items, reviews, and reports you submit.
  • Transaction records: item price, service fee, payment method, transaction status, and a masked reference to the payment method (for example, the last four digits of a card). We never store full card numbers or CVV codes.
  • Technical information: standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) generated automatically by any website request.

We deliberately do not ask for your home address, national ID number, or banking details directly. Payment is handled through the checkout flow described in our Refund Policy, and safe pickup locations are limited to public campus points rather than private addresses.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary:

  • Other students: your name, verification badge, profile photo, and listings are visible to other users, because that is how a peer-to-peer marketplace works. Your school email and student number are never shown to other students.
  • Payment processing: checkout details needed to process a payment are handled by our payment gateway. In this build that gateway is a sandbox simulation and no real payment data leaves the demo environment; when a live payment provider is connected, only the data required to process the transaction will be shared with it.
  • Hosting infrastructure: our hosting provider stores the application database and files needed to run the site.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law or a valid legal process.

5. Third-party resources loaded by this site

To render pages, Campus Swap loads the Bootstrap framework and Bootstrap Icons from the jsDelivr content delivery network. Loading any file from a CDN causes your browser to make a direct request to that provider, which will see your IP address and browser information as part of a normal HTTP request, the same way any website resource works. These are open-source code libraries only: they do not set tracking cookies and do not receive any of your Campus Swap account data. We do not currently use Google Fonts, Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or any other analytics or marketing script on this site. If that changes in the future, this section will be updated first, and separate consent will be requested where required.

6. Cookies

Campus Swap uses two strictly necessary cookies, both required simply to make the site function:

  • Session cookie: keeps you logged in as you move between pages. It is deleted when you log out or when your session expires.
  • CSRF protection cookie: a security token that prevents malicious websites from submitting forms on your behalf.

Neither cookie is used for advertising, analytics, or tracking you across other websites. Because these cookies are strictly necessary for the site to work, we do not show a cookie-consent banner. If Campus Swap ever adds non-essential cookies (for example, analytics or advertising), this section will be updated and consent will be requested before those cookies are set.

7. How long we keep your information

We keep account and transaction information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, tax, and dispute-resolution obligations. You can request deletion of your account as described in Section 8 below.

8. Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • ask us what personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or out of date;
  • ask us to delete your account and associated personal information, subject to records we are legally required to keep;
  • object to certain processing, such as marketing communications; and
  • lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator if you believe your information has been misused.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [INSERT PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].

9. Security

Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Forms are protected against cross-site request forgery. Card numbers and CVV codes are never written to our database. Access to administrative tools is restricted to authorised staff. No system is completely secure, and we encourage you to use a unique password for your account.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to active users before they take effect.
