Refund Policy

Last updated: [INSERT DATE].

1. Campus Swap is a peer-to-peer marketplace

When you buy an item on Campus Swap, you are buying it from another student, not from Campus Swap itself. We process the payment and hold the item in reserve for you, but the seller is responsible for the item's condition and for handing it over as described. This policy explains what happens at each stage of a transaction.

2. Before payment succeeds

When you choose to buy an item, it is temporarily reserved so no one else can purchase it while you complete checkout. If your payment is declined, or you cancel before paying, the reservation is released automatically and you are not charged.

3. After payment succeeds, before pickup

Once payment succeeds, the transaction status becomes PAID and the seller is notified to prepare the item for handover at one of the safe campus pickup points. If the seller does not show up, cancels, or the item turns out to be materially different from the listing, you can raise a dispute from your purchase history. An administrator will review the dispute and may authorise a full or partial refund back to your original payment method.

4. After the exchange is completed

Once both parties confirm the handover took place, the transaction is marked COMPLETED. Refund requests after completion are handled case by case through the dispute process and are not automatic, in the same way a private, in-person sale between two individuals would normally work. Nothing in this section limits any right you may have under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 that cannot lawfully be excluded.

5. How to raise a dispute

As a buyer, you can report:

  • the item was not received;
  • the item was significantly different from the listing;
  • the seller cancelled after payment; or
  • another transaction problem.

As a seller, you can report:

  • the buyer did not arrive for pickup;
  • the buyer cancelled; or
  • another transaction problem.

Open the transaction from "My Purchases" or "My Sales" and select "Report a Problem" to submit a dispute. An administrator will review the details from both sides before making a decision.

6. Refund timing and method

Where a refund is approved, it is returned to the original payment method used at checkout. Processing time depends on the payment method and, once a live payment provider is connected, on that provider's own settlement timelines. In this demo build, the payment gateway is a sandbox simulation, so no real funds are moved.

7. Donations and swaps

Donations are provided for free and are not refundable, since no payment is made. Swaps without a cash top-up involve no payment and therefore no refund process; if a swap included a cash top-up, only that top-up amount is eligible for a dispute-based refund under this policy.

8. Service fees

Where a Campus Swap service fee applies, it is shown to you in full before you confirm payment. If a transaction is fully refunded due to a valid dispute, the associated service fee is refunded along with it.

This document is a starting template and does not constitute legal advice. Before launching commercially, have it reviewed by a South African attorney to confirm it complies with the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 and any requirements specific to your chosen payment provider.
